# 📊 Finance Industry Analytics Dashboard (Google Looker Studio)

This project presents a full finance analytics dashboard created using **Google Looker Studio**, **Excel**, and **Google Trends** data.  
The dashboard provides a multi-dimensional view of customer behavior, loan performance, risk segments, product trends, marketing effectiveness, and repayment status.

This project is part of an advanced analytics workflow completed for Finance Industry analysis.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

The dashboard analyzes **Loan Amount**, **Credit Score**, **Interest Rate**, **Repayment Status**, **Customer Segment**, **Product Types**, **Branch Locations**, and includes **Google Trends Index** to understand market demand.

The visuals provide insights into:

- Loan risk profiles  
- Customer churn patterns  
- Product performance  
- Branch-wise behavior  
- Marketing channel effectiveness  
- Google Trends correlation with loan activity  
- Customer segmentation patterns  
- Credit Score distribution & impact

## 🔍 Key Insights

From the dashboard visualizations:

- **Marketing Channels:** Social media drives highest acquisitions (~37%).  
- **Product Types:** Loans lead in volume; Insurance is lowest.  
- **Google Trends:** Loan demand rises with online search interest. Strong leading indicator.  
- **Credit Score vs Loan Amount:** High loan values are issued mostly to mid-tier scores, not extremes.  
- **Branch Performance:** Branch interest rates vary significantly, suggesting uneven risk management.  
- **Repayment Status:** Nearly equal split between late/default/on-time, signaling balanced loan portfolio risk.  
- **Segments:** Salaried customers dominate high-value loans while students trend towards credit card and loan products.

---

## 🎯 Business Recommendations

- Optimize marketing spend toward **high-conversion channels** (Social Media).  
- Standardize **interest-rate policies** across branches to reduce discrepancies.  
- Promote **mortgage and insurance** products where demand lags.  
- Use **Google Trends** as a predictive tool for staffing and loan-officer allocation.  
- Support customers in “late/default” buckets with credit counseling or flexible repayment plans.
